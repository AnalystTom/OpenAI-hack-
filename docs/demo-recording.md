# Dots Office: two-minute demo

Open https://dots-office.pages.dev/ in Chrome. The public opening scene uses real, sanitized project recordings. For the live local feed, use http://127.0.0.1:3000 and select **Watch local Codex sessions → Current workspace**.

## Recording with OBS

Screen capture is configured for the built-in display. The MacBook camera overlay is enabled and the Mic/Aux meter showed input. Recording has not started.

1. Bring the Dots Chrome window to the front and maximize it. Keep the camera overlay clear of the controls you want to show.
2. Click **Restart all sessions**, leave replay at its default **1×** speed, then pause until ready.
3. In OBS, click **Start Recording**, return to Chrome, and resume the office. Record a short spoken test first; stop and play it back to check voice clarity and framing. OBS recommends checking your output settings and making a test recording: https://obsproject.com/kb/quick-start-guide.
4. Record the walkthrough below. Leave a short pause between sections so edits are easy. Finish with **Stop Recording** in OBS.
